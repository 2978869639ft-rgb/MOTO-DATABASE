# Project charter

MotoCMA-AU is a long-term personal motorcycle market intelligence platform for the
Australian used-motorcycle market. It assists the owner with research and judgement;
it does not replace that judgement.

## Engineering principles

- The GitHub repository is the canonical project source.
- Inspect and understand existing modules before every feature.
- Extend existing components instead of duplicating them.
- Prefer a simple, reliable design over clever or premature abstractions.
- Keep changes incremental, testable, documented, and working.
- Explain meaningful architectural trade-offs before major changes.
- Keep collection, standardisation, storage, CMA, valuation, and presentation
  independently evolvable.

## Data ownership

The owner is the only person permitted to permanently modify the research database.
Extraction and cleaning may prepare temporary drafts, but no listing is created or
updated without the owner's explicit approval.

## Product direction

Expected future capabilities include motorcycle standardisation, comparable market
analysis, fair-value estimation, historical price tracking, dashboards, trend
analysis, negotiation support, and reporting. These are roadmap directions rather
than permission to add complexity before it is needed.
